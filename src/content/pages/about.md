lede: Individuality is the universe discovering a new way to see itself.

We often hear that the universe is an ocean and we are the drops of water that make it. I believe
it is more than that. Drops are a poor analogy for people: they are interchangeable and
indistinguishable, and no single drop can move the ocean. One person can, toward creation or toward
destruction.

The highest expression of existence emerges when every being, in every form, is true to its own
nature. I have always wanted to unveil my full potential and bring something truly new into the
world. In the end, we are all forgotten, but what we make sets the world on a path it would never
have taken. What will you give back to the world you were given?
