## The problem

Much of what we can do in life depends on the people we know, yet we rarely see our own circle
clearly. A contact list keeps names. It does not show how people are connected, who you have lost
touch with, or who could introduce you to someone new.

## How it works

1. **Map** the people you know and how they know each other.
2. **Estimate** where you stand today in wealth, reach, and influence, each from 0 to 5.
3. **Analyze** the map. CircleLens looks at it through six lenses: goal fit, warm paths, bridge
   resilience, reciprocity, dormant ties, and network coverage.
4. **Act** on what it finds. Every suggestion shows its reasoning, such as a friend who could
   introduce you, or someone you have not spoken to in a while.

A suggestion is a reason to start a conversation, never a claim about what anyone intends or owes
you.

## The result

Relationships are laid out in their natural shape: who connects whom, where they hold, and where
they have gone quiet. Each suggestion carries its reasoning, so it can be weighed before acting. All
data stays in the browser, with JSON export for backups.
